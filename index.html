Privacy Policy

SA Bank Careers

Effective date: 16 November 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the independent individual publisher of SA Bank Careers handles information when you use the app.

SA Bank Careers is a directory that helps users find links to South African banks and their career websites. It does not process job applications or make employment decisions.

2. Information We Handle

The app does not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use its main features.

Search terms entered into the app are used to search the directory on your device.

If you save banks, your saved-bank preferences are stored locally on your device using the app's local storage.

3. Device Storage and Backups

Saved-bank preferences remain on your device until you remove them or clear the app's data.

Android app backup is enabled. Depending on your device and backup settings, app data may be included in device backups.

4. External Websites

The app contains links to external bank websites and other websites, including the South African Reserve Bank.

When you open an external website, that website's own privacy policy and terms apply. We do not control how those websites collect or use information.

5. Fonts and Internet Access

The app uses Google Fonts to display its text. Loading these fonts may involve a request to Google's servers, which may receive technical information such as your IP address.

The app requires internet access for external websites and online resources.

6. Permissions

The app's Android manifest declares internet access. It does not declare permissions for contacts, camera, microphone, location or SMS.

7. Children's Privacy

SA Bank Careers is an informational directory and does not knowingly request personal information from children. Users should seek guidance from a parent or guardian when appropriate before visiting external websites or sharing personal information online.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's features or information practices change. The effective date above will be updated when changes are made.
